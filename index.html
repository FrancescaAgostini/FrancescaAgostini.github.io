About me

I'm Francesca Agostini, a curious and committed person with a strong drive to learn and a willingness to step outside my comfort zone.

At the heart of my life choices has always been a deep passion for languages and cultural exchange, a passion that has led me to move between different cities over the years for study and work, and to build meaningful connections with people from a wide range of backgrounds. My academic and professional journey has been shaped by this same passion: over the years I've worked and studied to acquire solid linguistic and technical knowledge, staying up to date with the translation and localisation industry.

I have over three years of experience in the translation and localisation industry as a project manager coordinating multilingual projects for international enterprise clients, managing them end to end across languages, cultures, teams, and time zones.

In 2025, driven by curiosity about where translation technology is heading, I began a Master's degree in Communication and Translation Technology at the University of Geneva, aiming to deepen my technical knowledge, understand how technology is reshaping the language industry, and be able to actively participate in this transformation, instead of moving forward blindly.

I'm now nearing the end of my academic journey with a broader skill set that extends beyond translation and localisation into accessibility, website development, UX design, database management, and terminology technologies. I'm looking for my next challenge: a role where I can bring together my project coordination experience, technical skills, curiosity, and keep growing and learning every day.

Outside of work, I'm happiest outdoors: climbing at the crag, swimming in the sea, shooting analog film, going to the cinema or sharing a coffee with people close to my heart.
