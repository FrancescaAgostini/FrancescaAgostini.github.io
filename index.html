About me

I'm Francesca Agostini, curious, committed, and always ready to step outside my comfort zone. At the heart of my choices has always been a passion for languages and cultural exchange, which has taken me across cities, languages, and people over the years, and shaped both my career and my academic path.

I have over three years of experience in translation and localisation. At Translated, I worked as a project manager, coordinating multilingual projects for international enterprise clients end to end, across languages, cultures, teams, and time zones.

In 2025, driven by curiosity about where translation technology is heading, I began a Master's degree in Communication and Translation Technology at the University of Geneva, to deepen my technical knowledge and actively contribute to that transformation rather than simply adapting to it.

I'm now nearing the end of that journey with a broader skill set spanning machine translation, localisation, project coordination, translation and terminology technologies, accessibility, web development, UX design, and database management. I'm looking for a new role, where I can bring together my project coordination experience, technical skills, and curiosity.

Outside of work, I have (too) many interests. On sunny days, you'll find me outdoors climbing, swimming in the sea, running, doing yoga, or shooting analog pictures. On rainy days, you'll find me watching a movie or drawing animals on canvas.
