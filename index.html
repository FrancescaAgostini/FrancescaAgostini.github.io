About me

I am Francesca Agostini, a curious and committed person with a strong willingness to learn, embrace new challenges, and never afraid to step outside my comfort zone. I enjoy spending time outdoors, whether climbing at the crag, swimming in the sea, taking analog photographs, or having a coffee with people close to my heart.

I have over three years of experience coordinating multilingual projects for international enterprise clients, managing them from end to end and working across languages, cultures, teams, and time zones.

In 2025, driven by my curiosity about the latest developments in translation technology, I decided to pursue a Master’s degree in Communication and Translation Technology at the University of Geneva. My goal was to deepen my technical knowledge and explore how technology is transforming the language and localisation industry.

I am now approaching the end of my academic journey, with a broader skill set that goes beyond translation and localisation. Alongside my experience in project coordination, I have developed knowledge in website development, UX design, database management, terminology, and translation technology.

I am now looking for my next professional challenge. I am eager for an opportunity to bring together my experience, technical skills, and curiosity, and continue growing in this evolving field.
