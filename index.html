About me

I'm Francesca Agostini, a curious and committed person with a strong drive to learn and a willingness to step outside my comfort zone. Outside of work, I'm happiest outdoors: climbing at the crag, swimming in the sea, shooting analog film, or sharing a coffee with people close to my heart. I bring over three years of experience coordinating multilingual projects for international enterprise clients, managing them end to end across languages, cultures, teams, and time zones. In 2025, driven by curiosity about where translation technology is heading, I began a Master's degree in Communication and Translation Technology at the University of Geneva, aiming to deepen my technical knowledge and understand how technology is reshaping the language industry. I'm now nearing the end of that journey with a broader skill set that extends beyond translation and localisation — into website development, UX design, database management, and terminology work. I'm looking for my next challenge: a role where I can bring together my project coordination experience, technical skills, and curiosity, and keep growing in this fast-evolving field.
