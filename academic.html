Machine Translation projects

I conducted a comparative study evaluating the performance of a neural machine translation system (DeepL) against a generative AI translation system (Mistral) on a 300-word English-to-French text. Translation quality was assessed using the MQM (Multidimensional Quality Metrics) framework, providing a structured comparison of error types and overall output quality between the two approaches.

I conducted a research project examining how pre-editing (text normalisation) affects machine translation quality on community-generated content: text containing irregular punctuation, non-standard spelling, and culturally specific language. Using a 20-sentence English-to-French corpus, I compared human vs. LLM-based pre-editing and evaluated the correlation between automated metrics (BERTScore, COMET-Kiwi) and human quality assessments (SQM), including designing the LLM prompt used for automatic normalisation.

Translation Project Management projects

UX design projects

As part of a UX design course, I co-led the end-to-end UX design process for a plant care companion app, from initial research to a validated interactive prototype. I conducted user research, including client interviews, an online survey, and one-on-one interviews, to define user personas, scenarios, and a competitive analysis of existing products. Based on these insights, I designed paper prototypes and wireframes, detailing key features such as adding a plant to a collection, a care calendar, and a plant disease diagnostic tool. I then ran think-aloud usability evaluations and A/B testing, using SUS (System Usability Scale) scoring to identify improvement areas, and iterated the design into a high-fidelity interactive prototype with a full style guide and logo. Finally, I conducted a round of usability testing on the interactive prototype, using AttrakDiff to evaluate the user experience. You can find the Figma Prototype here (iPhone 17).
