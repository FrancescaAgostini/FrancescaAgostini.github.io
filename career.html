Main accomplishments as a Translation Project Manager

My first responsibility as a Translation Project Manager was managing a new enterprise account, a technology and software solutions provider expanding into five new markets. This required localising their website, marketing materials, and software interface into 5 languages. I owned the end-to-end localisation process, coordinating with IT on integration, with the quality team on resource selection, and with the programme manager on a shared cross-departmental Gantt chart. The launch, roughly 300,000 words across 5 languages, including full online implementation and LQA, was completed in under 2 months, and the client's satisfaction secured an ongoing partnership.

I subsequently built out the account structure, establishing a standard workflow and a dedicated project managers team, and took ownership of KPI tracking and monthly business reviews with the client.

Airbnb is one of the key accounts of my previous company, and a dedicated team of 5 project managers was established to handle their projects. I was nominated as part of this team and was responsible for managing the localisation of a specific Airbnb product across 64 markets. In this role, I held weekly meetings with the Airbnb Program Manager dedicated to the product, offering localisation consultancy, technical support, and monitoring of the product's overall localisation throughout the different stages of its development.

More and more companies are leveraging AI for translation purposes, but one of our clients, a leading global e-commerce and technology company, requested to establish a new process for handling AI data annotation projects. They used AI to generate content in 5 languages and requested professional linguists' expertise to review, rate, and annotate a great volume of words per week to improve their system and avoid semantic issues. Along with my manager, we were in charge of developing this new process from scratch. We carefully analysed the source materials that could be provided by the client, CSV files in this case, found a way to process that volume, and automated the process via regex and scripts using Google Apps Script. We also recruited a team of linguists, wrote guidelines, and trained them on the annotation process. Based on the linguists' feedback, we established rates and timelines. We monitored the quality of the deliverables, took notes on frequent mistakes, implemented PM quality checks in the process, as well as feedback loops with the linguists. The project was a success and became a weekly request.

I was assigned to coordinate a pilot project for a leading cloud monitoring and analytics company. The project involved 5,000 words of translation and 3 graphic flyers (DTP), all to be completed within 4 days. I managed resource recruitment, leveraging the linguists' time zones to maximise efficiency, and set up a dedicated communication channel to ensure linguistic consistency throughout the project. I also created a shared translation memory and glossary to harmonise translations in real time, and built a detailed schedule with dedicated steps for each day and task, to understand what could be done in parallel and what needed to be completed sequentially: copy extraction, CAT tool set up, translation, revision, DTP, QA, and feedback loops. The project was delivered successfully.
