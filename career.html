Experience

During my 6 months internship as an Account Manager at Translated, I worked closely with project managers and I was fascinated by their capability to turn any kind of request into real projects and deliverables. At the end of the internship, my manager noticed my interest in project management and offered me to apply for a project management position within the enterprise team. I was hired as an Associate Translation Project Manager (October 2022) and I have then been promoted to Translation Project Manager after less than 2 years (May 2024).

Main accomplishments as a Translation Project Manager (June 2024 – July 2025)

As a Translation Project Manager, I took on new responsibilities, focusing on client relationship management, KPI monitoring, and the development of new processes.

After the promotion, my first responsibility was to manage a new account, a technology and software solutions provider. They were looking to expand into five new markets and needed to translate and localise their whole website, marketing materials, and software interface into several languages. I was responsible for the end-to-end management of the localisation process, including coordinating with the IT team to find the best integration process, with the quality manager to select the appropriate resources for the type of content, and with the programme manager to establish a shared Gantt among the different departments. This first launch lasted about 2 months and included the translation of about 300,000 words into 5 languages, their online implementation and a whole LQA process. The client was satisfied with the results and decided to continue working with us for future projects.

From that moment, I was responsible for developing the whole structure of the cooperation with the client, including defining a standard workflow and creating a small project managers team to manage the different projects. I was also responsible for tracking KPIs and delivering monthly business review presentations along with the rest of the team to the client.

Main accomplishments as an Associate Project Manager (October 2022 – May 2024)

I have been managing challenging projects from day one, allowing me to shape and evolve my translation and localisation knowledge and project management skills every day.

As an Associate, I focused on production and coordination. I was responsible for the end-to-end management of translation projects: quotation and planning, selection and coordination of freelance linguists and professionals, resource management (guidelines, TMs, glossaries, instructions), quality assurance processes, and client communication.

Among my biggest achievements, along with my manager I led a complex web translation project for a leading Italian encyclopedia. They aimed to translate several online articles from Italian to English. To properly do so, all the abbreviations and acronyms needed to be made explicit. I was responsible for recruiting the team of linguists, creating the training materials, assigning tasks, and coordinating the project, ensuring that quality standards and deadlines were met.

Along the months, I developed a passion for audiovisual translation practices, including subtitling, dubbing, voice over (human and synthetic), on-screen text localisation and more. I worked on important marketing campaigns for a leading work management software company, which requested the full localisation (subtitling, voice over — human and synthetic — and on-screen text translation) of several short videos in more than 5 locales. I found room for improvement in the process (e.g. by identifying which steps could be done in parallel to improve time management) and drafted new guidelines and training material for the internal team. To showcase our work to the client, along with the account dedicated team, I presented our process and its strengths to the client, who was satisfied with the results and the quality of the deliverables, and decided to continue working with us for future projects.

In addition to translation and localisation, I was involved in SEO keywords research and implementation for a leading flight and travel comparison website. The process was completely new for me: I asked for guidance from senior project managers and marketing professionals, discovered the most recent and valuable tools for keyword research, and established a process dedicated to the client's request, involving specific tools, pivot tables and dedicated working sheets.
